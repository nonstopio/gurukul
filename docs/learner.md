# Learner guide

## Signing in

Open Gurukul and choose **Sign in with Google**. Only your `@nonstopio.com` account works; a
personal Gmail is signed straight back out with a message. If an Admin assigned you a course
before your first sign-in, it is waiting on your Home page.

## Taking a course

- **Home** shows the course you were last in, what is assigned to you (mandatory first, then by due
  date) and, if nothing is assigned, a link to the catalogue.
- **Catalogue** lists every published course. You can start any of them, assigned or not.
- A **course** has chapters (chapters) made of lessons and Quizzes. The outline shows each item as
  done, up next or locked. In a sequential course the next chapter opens when the previous one is done.
- In a **lesson**, press **Mark complete** to save and move on. Video lessons ask you to tick "I have
  watched the video"; reading lessons unlock the button when you reach the end. `J` and `K` move to
  the next and previous lesson.
- You can leave and come back on any device: **Resume** takes you to the lesson you were on.

## Quizzes (checkpoints)

A Quiz is a short quiz after a chapter. Answers are checked on the server, so the right answers
never reach your browser.

- One question per screen. Choose with the mouse or the keys `1`–`4` / `A`–`D`.
- Some Quizzes tell you after each answer whether it was right; others only at the end.
- The pass mark is shown at the bottom (70% unless the course says otherwise). If you do not pass
  you can retake it after a short wait; your best score counts.
- The result shows what to revisit, with links back to the lessons.

## Certificates

Finishing every lesson and passing every Quiz issues a certificate. Open **Certificates** to see
yours.

- **Download PDF** prints the certificate on one A4 page.
- **Add to LinkedIn** opens LinkedIn's add-certification form, filled in.
- **Copy verify link** gives a public link anyone can open, without signing in, to check the
  certificate is genuine. It shows your name, the course and the date, never your email.
